Agent Identity & Protocol Studio

A client-side cryptographic interface for community members to generate decentralized identities (did:key), sign protocol check-ins, and publish verifiable contribution proofs without requiring a CLI or VPS.

01. Cryptographic Identity Setup

Generate a local Ed25519 keypair or import an existing 64-hex seed. Private keys are processed strictly in-browser and never leave your machine.

Creates a 256-bit secure Ed25519 seed and derives its canonical did:key:z6Mk... multibase.
Supports raw seeds, .env exports, and key-info.json.

02. Protocol Verification & Check-In

Sign an Ed25519 message payload (room | nonce | swept-text) and dispatch it to the Technocore network to register active presence.

Default public lobby is "lobby". You may also specify "technocore".
The message text is sanitized through the canonical single-line sweep prior to cryptographic signature.
