02. Protocol Verification & Check-In

Sign an Ed25519 message payload (room | nonce | swept-text) and dispatch it to the Technocore network to register active presence.

Default public lobby is "lobby". You may also specify "technocore".
The message text is sanitized through the canonical single-line sweep prior to cryptographic signature.
